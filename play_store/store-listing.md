## Full description

Take a classic falling-block puzzle on the go with Flutris, a handheld-inspired arcade game. Stack tetrominoes, clear lines, and chase your best score across six distinct modes.

PLAY YOUR WAY

• Marathon — climb through 15 levels and clear 150 lines.
• Sprint 40 — race to 40 lines.
• Ultra 2:00 — score as much as you can in two minutes.
• Master — test your timing with instant gravity and a 300-line goal.
• Zen — play at a relaxed pace with no game over.
• Tactics — plan ahead with a deeper piece preview and placement hints.

BUILT FOR QUICK SESSIONS

• Responsive touch controls with a directional pad and action buttons.
• Ghost piece, hold, hard drop, and placement hints.
• Per-mode best scores saved on your device.
• Optional game sounds and haptic feedback.

Flutris works offline. It has no account sign-in, ads, analytics, or online score service.
